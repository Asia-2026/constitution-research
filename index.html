    Welcome to Living Constitution Research

    Welcome to the Living Constitution Research Archive — the official digital research repository of the Constitution Museum of India. This archive has been created to provide scholars, students, legal professionals, and citizens with comprehensive access to India’s constitutional history and its ongoing evolution.

    What We Cover#

    📜 Constitutional Commentary#

    Our commentary section provides in-depth analysis of key constitutional provisions, amendments, and their interpretations over time. Each article is carefully researched and written to provide historical context alongside legal analysis, making constitutional law accessible to both experts and general readers.

    ⚖️ Landmark Supreme Court Cases#

    This section documents the most significant Supreme Court judgments that have defined and shaped the Indian Constitution. From the Basic Structure Doctrine to Fundamental Rights jurisprudence, each case is presented with its background, arguments, judgment, and lasting constitutional impact.

    👤 Biographies#

    Explore the lives and contributions of the key figures who shaped India’s Constitution — from Dr. B.R. Ambedkar and Jawaharlal Nehru to the lesser-known but equally important members of the Constituent Assembly. Their vision and debates continue to guide constitutional interpretation today.

    📚 Constituent Assembly Debates (CAD)#

    The Constituent Assembly Debates are the primary source for understanding the intent behind every provision of the Indian Constitution. This section provides summaries, analysis, and direct references to the original debates that took place between 1946 and 1949.

    About This Archive#

    This research archive is maintained by the Advanced Study Institute of Asia (ASIA). Our mission is to make India’s constitutional heritage accessible, searchable, and understandable for everyone.
